# Privacy Notice · ITC Club Hub Version 1.0 · 2026-10-26

## 1. Who We Are
ITC Club Hub is operated by the Student Affairs Office and student club leaders at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC).

## 2. What We Collect and Why
We only collect the data necessary to run club events:
- **Student ID & Full Name:** To verify your enrollment and confirm your attendance at events.
- **Institutional Email:** To send your event registration confirmation and important updates.
- **Club Memberships:** To display your active clubs and allow leaders to communicate.
- **Phone Number (Optional):** If you opt in, we use this strictly to send urgent SMS event alerts (e.g., room change or cancellation).

We never collect your date of birth, photo, or national identity card.

## 3. Who Sees Your Information
- **Administrators:** Full access for official university reporting.
- **Event Organisers:** At event check-in, organisers only see your full name and a masked Student ID (`e2022****`). They never see your personal phone number or private email.
- We never sell or share your information with outside commercial sponsors.

## 4. How Long We Keep It
- **Account & Contact Data:** Retained while you are an active ITC student until graduation plus 6 months.
- **Event Attendance Records:** Kept for 1 academic year for reporting, then aggregated anonymously.
- **Phone Number:** Deleted immediately if you withdraw consent.

## 5. Your Choices & Rights
You can view your registered events at any time. Optional SMS reminders and email updates default to "OFF" and can be turned on or off anytime with zero penalty.

## 6. Contact Us
For any privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Student Affairs Office at: `clubhub-privacy@itc.edu.kh`.
