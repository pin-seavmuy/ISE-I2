# Licence Decision · ITC Club Hub Version 1.0 · 2026-10-26 · Lead: Scrum Master

## 1. Licence Comparison Matrix

| Criterion | MIT | Apache-2.0 | GPL-3.0 |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Anyone (including companies) may reuse | Yes | Yes | Only if entire work is GPL-3.0 |
| Improvements must be shared back (copyleft) | No | No | Yes (Strong copyleft) |
| Explicit patent grant | No | Yes | Yes |
| Compatible with GoogleTest (BSD-3-Clause) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Simple for future student teams to understand | Very simple (1 page) | Complex (multipage legal) | Complex rules & restrictions |
| Institute / Course preference | Highly recommended | Acceptable | Restrictive |

## 2. Decision and Rationale

**Decision:** We choose the **MIT Licence**.

**Rationale:**  
ITC Club Hub is an educational university project created by students. The MIT Licence provides the lowest barrier to adoption: it is short, universally understood, and allows future ITC cohorts and student clubs to study, reuse, and extend our codebase without complex legal restrictions. It is fully compatible with our test dependency, GoogleTest (BSD-3-Clause), and gives our team clear permission to showcase this project in personal portfolios.
